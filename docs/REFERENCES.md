# References and reading limits

Primary-source abstracts/records and author README consulted on 11 November 2026. Full requested PDFs were not successfully retrieved (403/502 responses); no claim of a complete full-text critical review. No upstream research model reproduced.

1. Marko Radisa Savic and Guoying Zhao. De-identification of facial videos while preserving remote physiological utility. BMVC 2023, paper 230. https://proceedings.bmvc2023.org/230/ ; authors: https://github.com/marukosan93/De-id_rPPG . A learned semi-adversarial approach combines biometric and physiological constraints. Our simple transforms are a baseline, not that model.
2. Hui Wei, Hao Yu and Guoying Zhao. Face De-Identification: A Domain-Centric Survey From Capture to Processing. IEEE TPAMI 48(11), 14578-14597, 2026. https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2026.3718681 ; https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/handle/10024/64955 . Physical, sensor and digital domains provide a broader context; our work concerns post-capture digital changes and separate privacy/utility evaluation.
3. Zitong Yu et al., including Guoying Zhao. PhysFormer: Facial Video-Based Physiological Measurement With Temporal Difference Transformer. CVPR 2022. https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/handle/10024/43584 . Temporal-difference attention motivates later learned extraction; no model run here.
4. Zhaodong Sun and Xiaobai Li. Contrast-Phys+: Unsupervised and Weakly-Supervised Video-Based Remote Physiological Measurement via Spatiotemporal Contrast. IEEE TPAMI 46(8), 5835-5851, 2024. https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2024.3367910 ; https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/handle/10024/48447 . Oulu-related label-efficient work, not coauthored by the two named supervisors. It motivates future self-supervised experiments, not training on one clip.
5. W. Wang et al. Algorithmic Principles of Remote PPG. IEEE TBME 64(7), 1479-1491, 2017. https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2016.2609282 . POS basis.
6. G. de Haan and V. Jeanne. Robust Pulse Rate From Chrominance-Based rPPG. IEEE TBME 60(10), 2878-2886, 2013. https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2013.2266196 . CHROM basis; local temporal implementation is simplified.
7. pyVHR: https://github.com/phuselab/pyVHR ; API: https://phuselab.github.io/pyVHR/pyVHR.BVP.html .
8. rPPG-Toolbox: https://github.com/ubicomplab/rPPG-Toolbox ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.00716 .
